Mackinac Island Fudge & Sweet Treats

At The Fudge Factory, we craft our legendary fudge using an original 1887 recipe from Martha Murdick of Mackinac Island. We also offer rich caramel corn, crunchy peanut brittle, and decadent chocolate-covered pretzels and cookies – all made fresh in-store for an unforgettable taste of tradition.

About Us

About The Fudge Factory

The Fudge Factory is a locally owned sweet shop carrying on the Mackinac Island fudge tradition, we're passionate about creating confections that delight every customer.

From our signature fudge to our handcrafted brittle and chocolate treats, everything we make reflects quality, tradition, and flavor you won't forget.

"My mission is to honor an iconic recipe and share our love for old-fashioned sweets with every visitor. We believe in making everything with care – just like Martha did in 1887."

Mark W. Aronoff Owner
