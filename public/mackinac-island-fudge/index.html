Traditional Mackinac Island Fudge by The Fudge Factory


Authentic • Handmade • Unforgettable


At The Fudge Factory, we take pride in creating authentic Mackinac Island Fudge using the original 1887 recipe. Each piece is rich, creamy, and carefully crafted to bring you a taste of tradition in every bite.
