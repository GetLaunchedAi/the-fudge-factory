About Us

Our Story

Crafting Sweet Memories Since Day One

The Fudge Factory is a locally owned confectionery dedicated to creating rich, creamy, and unforgettable fudge. We pride ourselves on using premium ingredients and traditional recipes to deliver flavors that bring people together.

From classic chocolate fudge to unique seasonal varieties, each batch is made fresh with love and attention to detail. Whether you're picking up a sweet treat for yourself or gifting someone special, we're here to make every moment a little sweeter.

I'm Mark W Aronoff, founder of The Fudge Factory. What started as a passion for making homemade fudge has grown into a beloved Plainwell, MI brand. Our mission is simple – to spread joy, one piece of fudge at a time.
